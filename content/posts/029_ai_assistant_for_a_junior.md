In [The Elder Scrolls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_V:_Skyrim) games, one can notice the following:

> Each skill has its own experience bar, which increases when the character performs an action in-game related to the skill.

This game mechanic feels quite natural, and it should be. We as humans are getting good at what we do on daily basis.
Try to remember something from your school classes that wasn't handy in real life. I have a hard time recollecting any historical lessons. History was never interesting for me, and I invested no time into it since.

I am wondering what will a Junior developer learn with an AI assistance. Will it be codding skills? Probably, he or she will excel in something different. In AI assistant usage, maybe.
There is nothing wrong in using AI for your advantages. I do a few copilot requests a day.

The question is: what would you like to be good at in the end ;)
